TMF vs ISF

TMF

- Maintained at the sponsor or CRO level.
- Contains essential trial documentation.

ISF

- Maintained at the investigator site.
- Contains site-level essential trial documents.

Practical Learning

- ISF document verification
- Document completeness checks
- Identification of missing documents
- Documentation review

Learning Objective

To understand clinical trial documentation, completeness, traceability and inspection readiness.
